Mock animation of Appalachian Demeter

On October 2nd, 2006, a group of teenagers claimed that one night, whilst going camping in the woods, they had seen a lanky, tall creature watching and following them.

After police digitally recreated the teenagers' description, they believe that this case, along with the disappearance of Jane Doe, are connected in some way.
