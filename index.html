Have You Seen This Person?

Highschool teenager Jane Doe, Potential Victim of The Appalachian Demeter

The people of the Appalachian mountains have claimed to see a long, lanky figure moving through the forests, blending in with the trees, and moving silently throughout the night.

They have given it the name of the 'Demeter', after a greek goddess of the same name, also known as 'The Goddess of Harvest'.
